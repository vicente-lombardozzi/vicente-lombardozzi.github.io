A clean indicator database and an **interactive dashboard** of Chilean economic and social indicators (2000-2023), built in **Plotly** and compatible with **Power BI**.

**Variables**: GDP, GDP per capita, unemployment, population, life expectancy and poverty, plus a regional breakdown (16 regions).

**Public sources**: World Bank, INE (Chile's National Statistics Institute), the CASEN 2022 survey and the Central Bank of Chile.

**What it shows**: building and maintaining a clean, consistent database and turning it into report-ready tables and charts. The file `chile_indicators_2000_2023.csv` opens directly in Power BI Desktop.

[Code, data and dashboard on GitHub](https://github.com/vicente-lombardozzi/vicente-lombardozzi.github.io/tree/main/projects/02_powerbi_chile)
