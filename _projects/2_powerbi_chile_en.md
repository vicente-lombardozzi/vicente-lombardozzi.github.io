Interactive dashboard of socioeconomic indicators for Chile (2000-2023), built with **Plotly** as an open-source equivalent to Power BI.

**Public data** from the World Bank, INE, CASEN 2022, the Chilean Central Bank and Our World in Data.

**Visualizations**: GDP, GDP per capita, life expectancy, unemployment, poverty by region (16 regions), regional Gini coefficient.

For recruiters who require Power BI: the file `chile_indicators_2000_2023.csv` can be opened directly in Power BI Desktop. Equivalent DAX queries are also included and documented.

[Code and dashboard on GitHub](https://github.com/vicente-lombardozzi/vicente-lombardozzi.github.io/tree/main/projects/02_powerbi_chile)
