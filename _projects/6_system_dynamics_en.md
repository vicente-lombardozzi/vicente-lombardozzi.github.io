**Python (scipy.integrate)** re-implementation of system-dynamics models I worked on in **Vensim** in the _Tools and Techniques_ course of my MSc (University of Leeds, 2019).

**Models**:

- **Savings-income**: capital accumulation and dynamic equilibrium
- **Lotka-Volterra**: two interacting populations, with phase plane
- **Renewable resource with extraction**: comparison of low and high extraction rates

**Why Python**: Vensim is closed, paid software; in Python the model stays open, version-controlled and reproducible.

[Code and original models on GitHub](https://github.com/vicente-lombardozzi/vicente-lombardozzi.github.io/tree/main/projects/06_system_dynamics_python)
